Drawn-Frame VR · Hilltop test

On Quest 3: tap Enter VR at the bottom. Put the controllers down to see your drawn hands.

Pinch (or pull the trigger) to switch between Layered and Flat. Flat puts every drawing on one distant backdrop, so you can feel what the layers add. Walk around inside the dashed circle.

Walk toward the girl by the wall: she greets you, then makes new items and sets them on the wall. Walk around her and the items: they are redrawn live for your angle. Get too close and she steps back.

On a computer: drag to look, WASD to walk, F to switch. Art sheet

Mode: Layered
