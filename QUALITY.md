# Art quality rubric

Target: high-end TV anime-fantasy art. Original characters and places only.
Each item is scored 1 to 5 from a render of the scene and the art sheet.

| # | Area | What a 5 looks like |
|---|------|---------------------|
| 1 | Background painting | Painted feel: soft gradients, brush texture, fine detail in foliage, stone, roofs. No flat fills. |
| 2 | Lighting and atmosphere | One clear key light, warm light and cool shadow, haze that softens distance, glow on bright areas. |
| 3 | Character proportions | About 7 heads tall, slim tapered limbs, natural weight and pose. |
| 4 | Character rendering | Hair in clumps with highlight band, 2 to 3 shading tones, detailed eyes, cloth folds. |
| 5 | Linework | Varied weight, tapered ends, line colour follows the area (brown on skin, soft lines in backgrounds). |
| 6 | Colour design | Harmonious palette, saturation controlled, character stands out from background. |
| 7 | Depth in the headset | Clear far, middle and near layers, believable scale, no cutout feel. |
| 8 | Consistency | Every piece and pose shares one style. |

## Where the remaining gap is

- Background painting (3/5): still reads as smooth digital shapes up close. Hand-painted detail (individual leaves, weathered stone, roof wear) is where code-drawn art stalls.
- Character rendering (3/5): one fixed front view. TV-quality needs turnarounds, cloth that drapes, and hand-drawn variation per pose.
- Expected ceiling for code-drawn art is around 28 to 30 out of 40. Painted art from an image model would be the route past that.
